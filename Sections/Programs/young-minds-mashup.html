Young Minds Mashup · Flagship Incubator

Where Innovation Meets Opportunity — Transforming Ideas into Impactful Startups

Are you brimming with innovative ideas? Do you dream of transforming your concepts into a successful startup? At YANC, we are here to make that happen. Young Minds Mashup is an exclusive incubation program designed to provide young entrepreneurs (aged 15–28) with the tools, mentorship, and funding needed to scale.

The 4 Core Incubation Pillars

1. Get Mentorship

Mentorship from seasoned industry experts and visionary founders to guide you through product design, market validation, and every step of the startup journey.

2. Access Funding

Funding opportunities, seed grants, and curated investor pitch stages designed to give wings to high-potential youth-led startup ideas.

3. Scale & Sustain

Learn robust unit economics, revenue architecture, and operational frameworks to scale and sustain your startup in the long run.

4. Collaborate & Network

Collaborate and network with high-agency, like-minded peers, co-founders, and ecosystem builders to exponentially accelerate your growth.
