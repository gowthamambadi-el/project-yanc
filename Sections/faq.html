Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YANC, membership, and programs.

What is a Discover Meet?

Discover Meet is a formal meet-and-greet session where prospective members receive an overview of YANC.

What exactly is YANC, and how is it different from other networking or youth clubs?

YANC is a unique networking platform designed to empower young minds by building meaningful connections and learning life skills from a young age. Unlike traditional groups, YANC focuses on experiential learning, mentorship, and personal growth.

How does YANC help young people beyond traditional education?

YANC bridges the gap between traditional education systems and real-world skills by focusing on life skills, networking, and mentorship opportunities that schools and colleges typically do not offer at scale.

Is YANC only for students, or can working professionals also join?

YANC is open to students, young professionals, and entrepreneurs aged 18–28 who are keen on learning, growing, and contributing to a supportive network.

How often do YANC members meet, and what happens in these meetings?

YANC members typically meet bi-weekly for structured discussions, mentorship sessions, networking, and interactive learning experiences.

Is YANC open to members from all backgrounds and fields?

Yes, YANC encourages a diverse community where members from various backgrounds and fields can connect and learn from one another.

What is the purpose of the membership application process?

The process is designed to evaluate, identify, and onboard driven young individuals whose values align with YANC's mission of purposeful learning, genuine connection, and real-world impact.

How can someone apply to join YANC?

Interested individuals can apply via the official website (www.yanc.in) by filling out the "Contact Us" or membership inquiry form. Our admissions team will reach out with the next steps.

Is there a selection process? If yes, what does it involve?

Yes. The process involves an application review, a personal interview, and a brief presentation on a designated topic to assess alignment and commitment.

What happens after someone gets selected as a YANC member?

Selected members receive an official welcome kit, complete onboarding orientation, are paired with a peer buddy, and join an active cohort for networking and development.

Is there a fee to join YANC? What does it cover?

Yes, a membership fee covers bi-weekly structured meetups, food & beverages, curated learning resources, mentor access, and community initiatives. Full financial details are walk-through during the Discover Meet.

Can parents enroll their children directly?

No. Youth members must apply and go through the selection process themselves to ensure personal motivation and commitment.

Can members join from any city?

Currently, YANC is based and operates in Hyderabad. Expansion into other major cities is underway.

If someone is not selected, can they reapply later?

Yes, candidates are encouraged to reapply in subsequent cohort cycles after a designated period.

When is the payment due after selection?

Once selected, you will receive an official payment link. The fee must be completed within one week to reserve and confirm your cohort seat.

What is the cost of YANC membership?

The membership fee is ₹4,500 per month, billed annually.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major payment methods including UPI, credit/debit cards, net banking, and verified digital wallets.

Are there any refunds if someone decides to discontinue?

Membership fees are non-refundable. In rare, exceptional circumstances, deferral to the subsequent cohort may be considered.

Does the membership fee cover all YANC events and programs?

The core fee covers all scheduled bi-weekly meetups, mentor sessions, resources, and regular networking events. Special external socials, retreats, or optional off-sites may involve separate registration fees.

What is the objective of the Young Minds Mashup funding process?

To establish a transparent, structured pathway for evaluating, funding, and incubating early prototype projects through the YANC Prototype Fund in partnership with Sri Venture Partners (SVP). The program provides funding and hands-on guidance for innovators aged 15–28 transitioning from ideation to prototype.
