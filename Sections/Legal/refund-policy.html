01 General Refund Policy Baseline

At YANC ENTERPRISES (“YANC”, “we”, “our”), we operate on cohort-based admissions with strictly limited physical and virtual seats. Due to the resource-intensive onboarding, exclusive mentor allocation, curriculum scheduling, and logistics commitment for each admitted cohort member, YANC enforces a strict no-refund baseline policy once services or cohort cycles have commenced.

02 Membership Fee Refunds

Cohort membership fees (₹4,500/month, billed annually) are strictly non-refundable once a membership seat is confirmed and onboarding credentials are provided.

Rare Permissible Exceptions:

  • Technical Duplicate Billing: Where a payment gateway error results in multiple charges for the same transaction.
  • Pre-Onboarding Application Rejection: If a candidate successfully makes a payment but fails final background verification before the cohort commencement date, a full refund will be initiated minus minor gateway processing fees.

Claims for exception reviews must be formally lodged in writing to accounts@yanc.in within 7 calendar days of the charge date.

03 Event & Workshop Fee Refunds

For standalone public masterclasses, external retreats, or special workshop tickets:

  • Events Cancelled by YANC: 100% full refund automatically credited to the original payment source within 10–15 business days.
  • Member Cancellation > 7 Days Prior to Event: Eligible for a 100% refund (subject to a ₹150 administrative gateway deduction).
  • Member Cancellation 3 to 7 Days Prior: Eligible for a 50% partial refund.
  • Member Cancellation < 3 Days Prior / No-Show: No refund permitted under any circumstances due to reserved venue seats and catering commitments.

04 Payment Errors & Duplicate Transactions

15-Day Logging Window: If you believe you were charged erroneously or noticed a duplicate debited amount, you must report the issue within 15 calendar days with your transaction reference ID and bank statement copy.

Send full payment reconciliation requests to accounts@yanc.in. Unclaimed duplicate charges after 15 days will be adjusted as future event credits.

05 Refund Processing Timeframes

Once an eligible refund is verified and approved by the YANC finance desk, the funds will be disbursed within 10 to 15 business days to the original payment method (Bank Account, UPI, Credit/Debit Card).

YANC is not liable for secondary delays caused by intermediary banks, payment aggregators, or public banking holidays.

06 No Refund for Misconduct or Violations

Non-Transferable & Zero Refund on Expulsion: Memberships are strictly personal and non-transferable to other individuals. If a member is suspended or expelled due to breaches of the Code of Conduct, no refunds or partial fee pro-rations will be granted.

07 Changes to This Policy

YANC ENTERPRISES reserves the right to revise or update this Refund & Cancellation Policy at its discretion. Any updates will become binding immediately upon being posted on our official portal.
