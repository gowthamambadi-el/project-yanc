01 Introduction & Purpose

This Cookie Policy explains how YANC ENTERPRISES (“YANC”, “we”, “our”) utilizes cookies, local storage tokens, and related web technologies when you browse or interact with www.yanc.in and affiliated member applications.

We believe in complete transparency regarding the information our platform saves on your device and how you can manage your preferences.

02 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer, tablet, or mobile phone by websites that you visit. They are widely used to ensure websites function properly, maintain secure user logins, remember display preferences, and provide analytical data to website operators.

Cookies can be “session cookies” (which are deleted once you close your web browser) or “persistent cookies” (which remain stored on your device until they expire or are manually cleared).

03 Categories of Cookies We Use

We classify cookies deployed on YANC platforms into four distinct categories:

3.1 Strictly Essential Cookies (Mandatory):

These cookies are vital for the core operation of our website. They facilitate secure user authentication, form submissions, and protection against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF). Without these cookies, services like application submissions and member login portals cannot function.

3.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies:

These cookies collect aggregated, anonymous telemetry regarding how visitors navigate our pages (e.g. popular cohort guides, bounce rates, interaction heatmaps). This data allows us to optimize load times and improve user experience.

3.3 Functionality & Preference Cookies:

These tokens allow the site to remember your specific settings—such as dark/light theme preferences, accordion expansions, and regional display options.

3.4 Campaign & Attribution Cookies:

Used strictly to measure the effectiveness of community outreach programs, university ambassador campaigns, and discover meet invitations.

04 Third-Party Cookies & Embeds

Some features on our website rely on verified third-party services that may set their own independent cookies. These include:

  • Google Analytics: Providing anonymous web traffic statistics and device breakdown reports.
  • Embedded Media & Fonts: Google Fonts CDN and social media previews (Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube).
  • Payment Processors: Secure verification tokens used during cohort fee settlements.

These third-party providers are governed by their respective privacy declarations.

05 Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the absolute right to choose whether to accept or block non-essential cookies. You can configure your browser settings to reject cookies or prompt you before accepting cookies:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and Site Permissions.
Please Note: Disabling strictly essential cookies may cause specific features (such as application draft saves or secure member logins) to malfunction.
